Straight answers

What does buyer representation cost me?

Usually nothing. In most purchases the buyer’s broker is paid from the commission the seller has already agreed to. I’ll explain exactly how it works for your boat before you sign anything.

Can you represent me on a boat listed with another broker?

Yes. Most yachts are listed for co-brokerage, so I can represent you on almost any boat on the market.

What size and price of yacht do you work with?

Yachts from $150,000 and up, for private clients anywhere in the world.

I already found a boat. Is it too late?

No. Send me the listing before you make an offer and I’ll give you my honest opinion, at no cost and no obligation.
