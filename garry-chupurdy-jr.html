A captain first, then a broker.

I’m Garry Chupurdy Jr. I’ve been running boats since 1998, brokering them since 2008, and I founded Sea Yachts in 2010. I’m licensed and bonded as a Florida yacht and ship broker (License EBK 7119) and hold a USCG captain’s license.

Sea Yachts is a small, hands-on brokerage. When you work with Sea Yachts, you work with me directly, from the first call to closing. I answer my own phone.

Over the years I’ve been a captain, broker, builder, rigger, painter and boat investor. I’ve restored and resold boats myself, so I can talk engines, hulls and survey findings as easily as titles and contracts.
