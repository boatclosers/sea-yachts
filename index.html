Straight answers

What does buyer representation cost me?

Usually nothing. In most purchases the buyer's broker is paid from the commission the seller has already agreed to. I'll explain exactly how it works for your boat before you sign anything.

Can you represent me on a boat listed with another broker?

Yes. Most yachts are listed for co-brokerage, so I can represent you on almost any boat on the market, including the ones in big-brokerage ads.

Why not just call the listing broker?

Because they work for the seller. Their job is to get the seller the highest price. Mine is to make sure you buy the right boat at the right price.

I'm not in Florida. Can you still help?

Yes. I work with clients around the world and specialize in exports. I can inspect boats, attend survey and sea trial, and manage closing and export on your behalf.

Do you also sell boats?

Yes. Every listing gets its own marketing campaign and a broker who can answer a buyer's hardest technical questions.
