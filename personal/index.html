About Garry

Garry Chupurdy Jr. is a licensed and bonded Florida yacht and ship broker and a USCG-licensed captain. He has been running boats since 1998 and brokering them since 2008, and in 2010 he founded Sea Yachts, a hands-on brokerage based in Palm Beach County.

Over the years Garry has worked as a captain, broker, builder, rigger, painter and boat investor. He has restored and resold boats himself, which is why clients rely on him for straight answers about engines, hulls, surveys and value.

At Sea Yachts, Garry represents yacht buyers, lists yachts for sale and helps clients around the world buy and export boats from Florida. He focuses on the part of a deal most people underestimate: titles, Coast Guard documentation, liens, sales tax, escrow and closing paperwork.

Garry lives and works in Lake Worth Beach, Florida.
